About New Red Oven

Real Pakistani flavour, cooked fresh in the heart of Shah Faisal Colony.

Who We Are

Authentic Food, Made to Order

New Red Oven Caterers & Biryani Center is based in Shah Faisal Colony No. 3, Karachi, specializing in Biryani, Live Smoke Biryani, BBQ, Tikka, Kabab, Karahi, Handi, Burgers, Sandwiches and Rolls.

From a single half-plate of biryani to a full family pack for a gathering, every order is prepared fresh — whether you're dining in, taking away, or having it delivered.

We also cater larger orders, including bulk degi biryani by the kilo, for gatherings and events.
